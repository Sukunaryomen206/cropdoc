Advanced Features

Every feature is designed with farmers in mind — simple, fast, and offline-ready.

Offline AI Detection

Detect diseases without any internet connection. The TensorFlow Lite model runs entirely on your device.

Early Disease Detection

Identify diseases at the very beginning — even when symptoms are still subtle and hard to notice.

Disease Severity %

Get Mild, Moderate, or Severe rating with an exact percentage to help you prioritize action.

AI Heatmap

Visually highlights the infected area on the leaf — shows exactly where the disease is spreading.

Organic Treatment Plan

Locally available organic solutions — neem, turmeric, garlic — with dosage and application schedule.

Disease Prediction

Forecasts disease risk for the next few days based on local weather conditions like humidity and rainfall.

Crop Health Score

Get an overall plant health score (e.g. 92/100) to understand your crop's condition at a glance.

Progress Tracking

Compare before and after photos to see recovery percentage and full treatment timeline.

Voice & Local Language

Voice guidance in Gujarati, Hindi, and English — accessible for all farmers regardless of literacy level.

Farm Dashboard

View total scanned plants, healthy vs diseased count, weekly analytics, and downloadable reports.
